What we are building

A community for independent car owners

SuryaCarPool brings together people looking for a self-drive rental and individuals who want to share a privately owned vehicle. Our focus is personal, owner-listed cars—not commercial fleet rentals.

Customers can browse the cars currently marked available, review the displayed rates, and submit booking details online. Owners can submit their contact information, car details, and supporting documents through the existing vehicle intake.

We aim to make the process easier to understand, with rental details and requirements presented as customers move through the booking experience.

Shanthappa Sappati, Founder & Managing Director. SuryaCarPool was established in October 2025.

Our principles
  • Respect the independent car owners behind each listing.
  • Show vehicle information and listed rates clearly.
  • Explain booking requirements before customers continue.
  • Build trust through transparent, accurate communication.
