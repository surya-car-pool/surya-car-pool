Self-Drive Booking Terms & Conditions

Customer must be at least 18 years old to book and drive the vehicle.

Required Documents

Driving License, Aadhar Card & Selfie photo are mandatory for identity verification.

Documents are required only once for your first booking.

Important: Documents must be uploaded and approved before pickup time. Failure will result in automatic cancellation with no refund.
Refund Policy:
Full refund available if booking is cancelled at least 3 hours before pickup time. Cancellations within 3 hours are non-refundable.
Customer Responsibilities:
  • All traffic challans / fines
  • All toll gate charges
  • Fuel expenses during rental
Security Deposit Requirement:

At the time of vehicle pickup, the customer must provide any one of the following as a security deposit:

  • One Bike (two-wheeler)
  • Original Driving License
  • Original Passport
  • Cash deposit of ₹10,000 (Indian Rupees)

The security deposit will be returned after the vehicle is safely returned without damages or pending dues.

Vehicle Usage Rules

  • No sub-leasing or commercial usage
